History of the CPSU(b). Short Course

Комиссия ЦК ВКП(б) — История ВКП(б). Краткий курс (History of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks). Short Course). Approved by the Central Committee of the CPSU(b) in 1938; this printing, OGIZ / Gospolitizdat, Moscow, 1945. A reprint of the canonical Stalin-era party history.

AuthorCollective commission of the Central Committee of the CPSU(b) — the official party history issued under Stalin’s direct editorship (commonly attributed to Stalin and the Central Committee commission); approved by the CC CPSU(b) 1938
Edition1945 reprint (OGIZ / Gospolitizdat, Moscow) of the text first approved in 1938; FB2 electronic edition (librusec-id 655522) prepared 2018
StructureIntroduction + 12 chapters + Conclusion; canonical Short Course ending; 4,703 lines / ~810,000 characters of extracted text
Period1883–1937: from the rise of Russian social democracy to the completion of socialist construction and the adoption of the 1936 Constitution
NoteIncludes the Stalin “Antaeus” parable from On the Shortcomings of Party Work; Lenin quotations cited by volume and page (т. XXV, XXVII)

Contents. The canonical Short Course of party history. The introduction frames the study of party history as the study of the struggle of the revolutionary proletariat; the work then narrates the formation of Russian social democracy from the abolition of serfdom and the rise of the industrial proletariat, the narodnik movement and Plekhanov’s Emancipation of Labour group, the beginning of Lenin’s revolutionary activity, and the creation of the “Iskra” newspaper. It treats the formation of the RSDRP at the Second Congress and the split into Bolsheviks and Mensheviks; the party in the Russo-Japanese War and the first Russian revolution (Bloody Sunday, the Potemkin mutiny, the October general strike, the December armed uprising, the Duma); the party in the Stolypin reaction and its formation into an independent Marxist party at the Prague conference; the party in the years of the labour upsurge before the First World War; the party in the imperialist war and the February revolution; the preparation and conduct of the October socialist revolution; the party in the period of foreign military intervention and civil war; the transition to peaceful work and the restoration of the national economy; the struggle for socialist industrialization; the struggle for the collectivization of agriculture; and the completion of the construction of socialist society with the adoption of the new Constitution. The conclusion states the chief lessons of the historical path of the Bolshevik party.

Character of the source. The official Soviet party history of the Stalin period, approved by the Central Committee in 1938 and for decades the obligatory text of party and civic education. Written from a strict Bolshevik standpoint, it presents the party’s history as the history of the struggle of the Marxist-Leninist line against its internal opponents (economists, Mensheviks, Trotskyists, “rightists,” Bukharinists) and against the enemies of the revolution. Its chapter structure — twelve chapters from 1883 to 1937 — is the authoritative periodisation of Soviet history adopted throughout the Stalin era. A companion volume in this folder — Shestakov’s Short Course of the History of the USSR (1937), a school textbook covering the same years — has its own source page.

Введение (Introduction)

The study of the history of the party of the Bolsheviks as the study of the struggle of the revolutionary proletariat; the party of a new type; the worldwide significance of the history of the CPSU(b).

Глава I. Борьба за создание социал-демократической рабочей партии в России (1883–1901 годы)

The struggle for the creation of a social-democratic workers’ party in Russia, 1883–1901. The abolition of serfdom and the development of industrial capitalism in Russia; the appearance of the modern industrial proletariat; the first steps of the workers’ movement. Narodism and Marxism in Russia; Plekhanov and his “Emancipation of Labour” group; Plekhanov’s struggle against narodism; the spread of Marxism in Russia. The beginning of Lenin’s revolutionary activity; the Petersburg “Union of Struggle for the Emancipation of the Working Class.” Lenin’s struggle against narodism and “legal Marxism”; the Leninist idea of the alliance of the working class and the peasantry; the First Congress of the RSDRP. Lenin’s struggle against “economism”; the appearance of the Leninist newspaper “Iskra.”

Глава II. Образование Российской социал-демократической рабочей партии. Появление внутри партии фракций большевиков и меньшевиков (1901–1904 годы)

The formation of the RSDRP and the appearance within the party of the Bolshevik and Menshevik factions, 1901–1904. The rise of the revolutionary movement in Russia in 1901–1904. The Leninist plan of building a Marxist party; the opportunism of the “economists”; the struggle of “Iskra” for the Leninist plan; Lenin’s book What Is to Be Done?; the ideological foundations of the Marxist party. The Second Congress of the RSDRP; the adoption of the programme and statute and the creation of a united party; disagreements at the congress and the appearance of two currents in the party — Bolshevik and Menshevik. The splitting actions of the Menshevik leaders and the sharpening of the struggle inside the party after the Second Congress; the opportunism of the Mensheviks; Lenin’s book One Step Forward, Two Steps Back; the organisational foundations of the Marxist party.

Глава III. Меньшевики и большевики в период русско-японской войны и первой русской революции (1904–1907 годы)

The Mensheviks and Bolsheviks in the period of the Russo-Japanese War and the first Russian revolution, 1904–1907. The Russo-Japanese War; the further rise of the revolutionary movement; strikes in Petersburg; the demonstration of workers at the Winter Palace on 9 January 1905; the shooting of the demonstration; the beginning of the revolution. Political strikes and workers’ demonstrations; the growth of the peasant revolutionary movement; the mutiny on the battleship “Potemkin.” Tactical disagreements between Bolsheviks and Mensheviks; the Third Congress; Lenin’s Two Tactics of Social-Democracy in the Democratic Revolution; the tactical foundations of the Marxist party. The further rise of the revolution; the all-Russian political strike of October 1905; the retreat of tsarism; the tsar’s manifesto; the appearance of Soviets of Workers’ Deputies. The December armed uprising and its defeat; the retreat of the revolution; the First State Duma; the Fourth (Unity) Congress. The dissolution of the First Duma; the convocation of the Second Duma; the Fifth Congress; the dissolution of the Second Duma; the causes of the defeat of the first Russian revolution.

Глава IV. Меньшевики и большевики в период столыпинской реакции. Оформление большевиков в самостоятельную марксистскую партию (1908–1912 годы)

The Mensheviks and Bolsheviks in the period of the Stolypin reaction; the formation of the Bolsheviks into an independent Marxist party, 1908–1912. On dialectical and historical materialism. The Bolsheviks and Mensheviks in the years of the Stolypin reaction; the struggle against the liquidators and otzovists. The Bolshevik struggle against Trotskyism; the August anti-party bloc. The Prague party conference of 1912; the formation of the Bolsheviks into an independent Marxist party.

Глава V. Партия большевиков в годы подъема рабочего движения перед первой империалистической войной (1912–1914 годы)

The party of the Bolsheviks in the years of the upsurge of the workers’ movement before the first imperialist war, 1912–1914. The rise of the revolutionary movement in 1912–1914. The Bolshevik newspaper “Pravda”; the Bolshevik faction in the Fourth State Duma. The victory of the Bolsheviks in the legal organisations.

Глава VI. Партия большевиков в период империалистической войны. Вторая революция в России (1914 – март 1917 года)

The party of the Bolsheviks in the period of the imperialist war; the second revolution in Russia, 1914 – March 1917. The origin and causes of the imperialist war. The theory and tactics of the Bolshevik party on questions of war, peace and revolution. The defeat of the tsarist troops at the front; economic ruin; the crisis of tsarism; the February revolution.

Глава VII. Партия большевиков в период подготовки и проведения Октябрьской социалистической революции (апрель 1917 – март 1918 года)

The party of the Bolsheviks in the period of the preparation and conduct of the October socialist revolution, 1917. The beginning of the crisis of the Provisional Government; the April conference of the Bolshevik party. The party’s course on the preparation of the armed uprising; the Sixth Congress. The Kornilov plot and its defeat; the transfer of the Soviets in Petrograd and Moscow to the side of the Bolsheviks; the October armed uprising. The struggle for the consolidation of Soviet power; the Brest peace; the Seventh Congress.

Глава VIII. Партия большевиков в период иностранной военной интервенции и гражданской войны (1918–1920 годы)

The party of the Bolsheviks in the period of foreign military intervention and civil war, 1918–1920. The beginning of the foreign military intervention; the first period of the civil war. The military defeat of Germany; the revolution in Germany; the formation of the Third International; the Eighth Congress. The attack of the Polish gentry on the Soviet country; the sortie of General Wrangel; the failure of the Polish plan; the rout of Wrangel; the end of the intervention.

Глава IX. Партия большевиков в период перехода на мирную работу по восстановлению народного хозяйства (1921–1925 годы)

The party of the Bolsheviks in the period of the transition to peaceful work on the restoration of the national economy, 1921–1925. The Soviet country after the liquidation of the intervention and civil war; the difficulties of the restoration period. The discussion in the party on the trade unions; the Tenth Congress; the defeat of the opposition; the transition to NEP. The first results of NEP; the Eleventh Congress; the formation of the USSR; Lenin’s illness; Lenin’s co-operative plan; the Twelfth Congress.

Глава Х. Партия большевиков в борьбе за социалистическую индустриализацию страны (1926–1929 годы)

The party of the Bolsheviks in the struggle for the socialist industrialisation of the country, 1926–1929. The difficulties in the period of socialist industrialisation and the struggle with them; the formation of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist anti-party bloc; the anti-Soviet pronouncements of the bloc; the defeat of the bloc. The successes of socialist industrialisation; the lag of agriculture; the Fifteenth Congress; the course on the collectivisation of agriculture; the rout of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc; political double-dealing. The offensive against the kulaks; the Bukharin-Rykov anti-party group; the adoption of the first five-year plan; socialist emulation; the beginning of the mass collective-farm movement.

Глава XI. Партия большевиков в борьбе за коллективизацию сельского хозяйства (1930–1934 годы)

The party of the Bolsheviks in the struggle for the collectivisation of agriculture, 1930–1934. The international situation in 1930–1934; the economic crisis in the capitalist countries; the seizure of Manchuria by Japan; the coming of the fascists to power in Germany; two seats of war. From the policy of restricting the kulak elements to the policy of liquidating the kulaks as a class; the struggle with distortions of the party’s policy in the collective-farm movement; the offensive against the capitalist elements on the whole front; the Sixteenth Congress. The plan for the reconstruction of all branches of the national economy; the role of technology; the further growth of the collective-farm movement; the political departments of the machine-tractor stations; the results of the fulfilment of the five-year plan in four years; the victory of socialism on the whole front; the Seventeenth Congress. The degeneration of the Bukharinites into political double-dealers; the degeneration of the Trotskyist double-dealers into a White-guard band of murderers and spies; the villainous murder of S. M. Kirov; the measures of the party to strengthen Bolshevik vigilance.

Глава XII. Партия большевиков в борьбе за завершение строительства социалистического общества и проведение новой Конституции (1935–1937 годы)

The party of the Bolsheviks in the struggle for the completion of the construction of socialist society and the adoption of the new Constitution, 1935–1937. The international situation in 1935–1937; the temporary easing of the economic crisis; the beginning of a new economic crisis; the seizure of Abyssinia by Italy; the German-Italian intervention in Spain; the invasion of Central China by Japan; the beginning of the second imperialist war. The further rise of industry and agriculture in the USSR; the early fulfilment of the second five-year plan; the reconstruction of agriculture and the completion of collectivisation; the significance of cadres; the Stakhanovite movement; the rise of popular welfare; the rise of popular culture; the strength of the Soviet revolution. The Eighth Congress of Soviets; the adoption of the new Constitution of the USSR. The liquidation of the remnants of the Bukharinist-Trotskyist spies, wreckers and traitors to the motherland; the preparation for elections to the Supreme Soviet of the USSR; the party’s course on developed intra-party democracy; the elections to the Supreme Soviet of the USSR.

Заключение (Conclusion)

The chief lessons of the historical path of the Bolshevik party: the party of the working class is the leading force of the revolution; the party grows stronger by cleansing itself of opportunist elements; the party is strong through its bond with the people, who are the source of its strength. Includes the “Antaeus” parable. Text ends: “Such are the chief lessons of the historical path traversed by the Bolshevik party.”
